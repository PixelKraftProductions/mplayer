mPlayer: File Manager & Player Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026
Last updated: 28 October 2026

mPlayer (“mPlayer”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is an offline-first media player and file-management application published by Pixel Kraft Productions. This Privacy Policy explains what information mPlayer accesses, stores, uses, shares, and deletes when you use the app.

By “device” we mean the phone, tablet, or other device on which mPlayer is installed. This policy applies to the current Android and iOS versions of mPlayer and to information handled by the app itself. Third-party apps and services that you choose to use through mPlayer have their own privacy policies.

1. Summary

  • mPlayer does not require an account or maintain an mPlayer cloud account.
  • Your media library and local file index stay on your device during normal use.
  • The current version does not display advertising or use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Sharing, backup export, support email, store links, and other external actions happen only when you choose them.
  • You control the storage folders and files that mPlayer can access.

2. Information mPlayer accesses and stores

Files and folders

When you grant storage access or choose a folder, mPlayer may access the files and directories needed for browsing, searching, categorizing, playing, renaming, copying, moving, deleting, sharing, favoriting, trash, and restoring files. On Android, the file-manager feature may request All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) because its core purpose includes scanning and managing user-selected shared-storage files and folders across locations, including documents, archives, and APKs. This access is used for mPlayer’s own file-management features, not for advertising or another service. The local index may contain file and folder names, paths or document identifiers, file types/MIME types, sizes, timestamps, categories, and related status information. mPlayer does not need to upload your media library to provide these features.

Local app data

mPlayer stores preferences, language and theme choices, favorites, playlists, playback history, session information, reminders, library counts, local database records, Private Vault metadata, encrypted Vault files, and temporary files needed to preview, play, import, export, or share a file.

Vault PIN and biometrics

If you enable Private Vault, mPlayer stores the Vault configuration and a protected representation of the PIN in app-private storage. Vault files are encrypted into app-private storage. Biometric authentication is performed by the operating system; mPlayer does not receive or store fingerprint or face data.

Support messages

Help & feedback opens your device’s email app. The draft may include the message you type and basic app version/build information. Nothing is sent until you review and send it.

3. How information is used

mPlayer uses local information to scan, index, search, organize, and play media; remember preferences and library state; perform file actions you request; protect files in Private Vault; create or restore a backup you request; and prepare sharing or feedback actions you choose. mPlayer does not use local media or metadata for advertising profiling or sale to data brokers.

4. Sharing and disclosure

mPlayer does not upload your media library or local index to an mPlayer server in the current version and does not sell personal information. Information may leave the app through the operating-system share sheet, a user-selected backup/export destination, the email app, app stores, external links, or other system services you choose. The relevant recipient or provider’s own terms and privacy policy apply.

The current production configuration does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, social login, or an mPlayer cloud backend. If this changes, this policy and applicable store disclosures will be updated.

5. Permissions and access choices

Depending on platform, device, and feature, mPlayer may use file/directory or storage access, including Android’s All files access setting for its broad file-manager workflows; Internet access for links and external services that you open; and biometric/device authentication when you choose protected features. You can deny or revoke permissions in device settings; some File Manager features may stop working. Revoking All files access does not automatically delete the local index, files, or copies you previously shared or moved. mPlayer does not upload files or the file index merely because All files access is enabled.

6. Retention and deletion

Local settings, library indexes, playlists, favorites, history, reminders, and other app records remain on the device until you remove them, clear the relevant feature, clear app data, or uninstall mPlayer. Vault files remain until restored, deleted, or erased. Uninstalling normally removes app-private Vault storage. Ordinary shared-storage files, exported backups, shared copies, and files moved elsewhere are not necessarily deleted when mPlayer is removed. Since mPlayer has no online account or server-side personal-data store, there is no server account to delete.

7. Security

mPlayer uses app-private storage and platform security controls for protected data, and its Vault is designed to encrypt selected files on the device. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device updated and maintain independent copies of important files.

8. Children’s privacy

mPlayer is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information for an online account or advertising purpose.

9. International use

Core library and playback data is designed to remain on your device. Email, sharing, backup destinations, app stores, and other external services may process information under their own policies and applicable law.

10. Your choices and rights

You can choose folders, revoke permissions, delete local records or files, clear app data, uninstall the app, decline sharing, cancel exports, and choose whether to send support email. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing. Contact us for privacy questions or requests.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date identifies the current version.
